## 1. What is this study?

Our team is building an AI assistant that helps junior maintenance technicians fix factory
machines using notes left by experienced technicians. To judge whether it helps, we compare it with
people doing the same tasks by hand. You are one of those people. **We are testing the AI system,
not you.** No experience with machines is needed.

## 2. What will I do?

The session takes about **60 minutes** in one sitting:

1. A 5-minute briefing and a practice question.
2. **Diagnosis task (about 35 minutes):** 12 short repair questions. For each, search a
   collection of past repair notes and write down the likely causes, the repair steps and any
   safety precautions. Each question is timed, with a 3-minute limit.
3. **Extraction task (about 15 minutes):** read two repair notes and fill in a structured form.
4. A 2-minute questionnaire about your background (no names, only broad categories).

All machines, technicians and repairs in the materials are **synthetic (made up)**.

## 3. What data is collected?

- Your written answers and how long each task took.
- Broad background only: study area and any hands-on mechanical or electrical experience
  (none / some / a lot).
- **Not collected:** your name on any answer sheet, your student ID, photos, audio or video.

Your answers are labelled with a code such as `P03`. Only the study lead keeps the list linking
codes to this consent form, on paper or in the ignored `private/` folder. It is destroyed after
the competition finale (7 December 2026).

## 4. How will the data be used?

Anonymised answers are graded blind: graders see shuffled answers from people and from AI
systems without knowing which is which. Results appear only as group statistics (for example
average time per question) in the team's competition report and presentation. No individual
can be identified.

## 5. Your rights

- Taking part is **voluntary** and unpaid.
- You may skip any question, take a break, or stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You may ask for your answers to be removed until **28 November 2026, 11:59 PM**, when the
  results are anonymised and merged. After that they cannot be traced back to you.
- There are no known risks. The tasks involve only reading and writing.
